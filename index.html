About the Expo

Welcome to Autovent '26

Autovent is the premier automotive expo of the decade, bringing together the world's most iconic brands, visionary engineers, and passionate enthusiasts under one roof in Los Angeles.

From electric vehicle innovations to timeless classic restorations, Autovent '26 celebrates every facet of the automotive tapestry — blending heritage with the future of mobility.
